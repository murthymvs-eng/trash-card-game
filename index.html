How to Play Trash

  1. On your turn, click the deck to draw 1 card, then drag up to 2 cards from your hand onto the matching empty slots.
  2. A card fits only in its own rank's slot — except the Jack (J), a wildcard that fits any empty slot. Queens and Kings are useless — they can never be placed.
  3. First to fill all 10 slots wins instantly.
  4. If the deck runs out first, the player with the most cards placed wins.
